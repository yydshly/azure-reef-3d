# Camera-height research

Four real Chromium/SwiftShader images of unchanged production a18e1f7. Only eye height varies. Midway and route75 are each paired within one runner; the pairs come from two runs after a test roundoff failure. Raw failed and successful reports are preserved. Summary rechecks all four existing captures with the same .001 camera tolerance. No production, route or performance claim.

## Visual decision

Owner and independent review passed the limited static experience gate: midway shows clearer side walls/overhangs and fish at eye level. The 75% view is neutral to mildly improved, with more open water and no new occlusion. A continuous path still needs separate validation. No production release, overall naturalness or performance acceptance follows from these four images.
