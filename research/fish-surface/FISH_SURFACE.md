# One generic fish surface candidate — not published

Accepted baseline source 6750ba68 / public a18e1f7. Only fish_03 is refined, after fish_05 has already been cloned. The body geometry and original envelope remain; new fin points stay within the old prisms. Other fish, the reef, route, camera, guide, lights and water are unchanged. Body pigmentation is evaluated continuously instead of interpolating hard per-vertex choices. Five closed fins are thinner with restrained original ray-like shading. This is not species identification or complete realistic anatomy.

The offline side/reverse comparison visibly reduces the pigment zigzags but retains angular silhouettes and existing eyes. Actual browser review is still required. Inspect the unchanged midpoint at 47° FOV and two side diagnostics at 20° inspection magnification, same phase 0 and light. The close views are QA magnification, not a currently exposed user preset. camera/occlusion/control preflight reports are in evidence/fish-surface-oct7.

Only new geometry is 980 extra triangles on one fish; mesh/object/fish counts and original bounds are unchanged. Runtime performance remains unverified. Source checks verify actual GLTF resource isolation, body bytes, transforms, topology, shader assembly, 625 articulation/heading support samples per fin and the original route/control behaviors. These checks do not replace WebGL compilation or visible quality review.

References are linked in reference-audit.json. Museum photographs/diagram were viewed but not copied into textures or archived in this deliverable. All pigment/fin construction is original code; no imported scan, photo texture, paid model or new account is involved.
