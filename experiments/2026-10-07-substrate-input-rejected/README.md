# Coral Ground 02 — input suitability rejection

The original 4K diffuse image was downloaded and visually inspected. It contains recognizable grass leaves and terrestrial surface debris. The source describes exposed coral ground, not a submerged living reef surface. Its CC0 license permits asset use, but that does not establish ecological suitability. The asset was not applied to any model, material, webpage or deployment. No WebGL comparison was claimed.

Author: Rob Tuytel. Source: https://polyhaven.com/fa/a/coral_ground_02 . License: https://polyhaven.com/fa/license . The page declares a 2 m texture width; the measurement method was not independently verified. Three downloaded maps and their exact hashes are recorded in input-manifest.json. Large originals remain outside the source archive.

This rejects this input for direct use as underwater bare hardbottom. It does not reject all photogrammetry assets or claim that no suitable underwater data exist. Future candidates require actual pixel/geometry inspection, usable license, scale/provenance and a bounded fixed-scene visual comparison.
